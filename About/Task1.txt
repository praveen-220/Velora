11.  AC PRICE BONUS

If AC enabled:

+₹1/km
1️2️.  FINAL PRICE EXAMPLE

Example:

Distance = 400 km
Car = Sedan
Sedan Price = ₹7/km
AC = Enabled (+₹1/km)

Final price/km = ₹8

Calculation:

400 × 8 = ₹3200

If seats = 4

₹3200 / 4 = ₹800 per passenger
